How CIPHER Solves the Core Scientific Research Crisis

The Traditional Research Bottleneck

  • Cognitive Overload: Researchers spend 40+ hours per month reading repetitive PDFs, manually copying tables, and hunting for dataset references.
  • Public LLM Hallucinations: Standard chatbots invent fictitious papers, fabricate DOIs, and misrepresent empirical accuracy metrics.
  • Fragmented Information Silos: Cross-document relationships (e.g., Paper A contradicting Paper B's baseline) remain completely hidden in disconnected PDF files.
  • Single-Perspective Blindspots: Without multi-agent adversarial peer review, statistical weaknesses and evaluation flaws slip through undetected.
  • Tedious Report Drafting: Writing comprehensive literature reviews and deployment manuals is manual, repetitive, and prone to omission.

The CIPHER Agentic AI Solution

  • Instant Multi-Paper Ingestion: Automated PDF, DOCX, TXT, and Web parsing with 384-D dense vector embedding and spaCy entity tagging.
  • Strict Verbatim Grounding: Knowledge Copilot returns exact passage excerpts, document source names, and mathematical cosine scores.
  • Interactive Knowledge Graph: Cytoscape engine visually exposes cross-document links, shared methodology hubs, and citation networks.
  • 4-Agent Collaborative Flow: Autonomous Researcher, Reviewer, Experiment Designer, and Statistician simulate a full research lab.
  • Automated Report Studio: Instantly compiles publication-ready literature reviews, executive briefs, and SOPs with markdown export.

Complete Feature-by-Feature Guide & Instructions
01

1. Knowledge Library

Secure Document Ingestion, 384-D Tensor Vector Indexing & NER Processing
Foundation Module
Problem It Solves

Manual reading and filing of unstructured research documents is slow, fragmented, and unsearchable. Raw files cannot be queried with semantic precision by standard search.

CIPHER Solution: Automatically parses text structure, extracts named entities, chunks passages, and generates dense vector embeddings stored in a local SQLite database.

How to Use It
1 Navigate to Knowledge Library from the sidebar.
2 Drag and drop or browse to select your research files (PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD, CSV).
3 Click Upload & Index. The live progress bar monitors Parse → Chunk → Entity extraction.
4 View the ingested document cards showing format badges, word count, passage count, and indexed status.
02

2. Import & Web Research

Automated Web Ingestion, Clean HTML Parsing & Real-Time Vector Storage
Web Scraper
Problem It Solves

Valuable research findings, preprints, and engineering technical blogs are trapped behind web clutter, ads, navigation scripts, and non-standard HTML layouts.

CIPHER Solution: Cleans HTML via DOM filters, extracts main body text, computes dense embeddings, and injects the article directly into your indexed project workspace.

How to Use It
1 Open Import & Web Research.
2 Paste any scientific article, blog, or documentation URL into the input field.
3 Click Fetch & Analyze.
4 Review the clean article excerpt, word count, and passage embedding confirmation. It is now immediately available to Copilot and Evidence Explorer.
03

3. Knowledge Copilot (Source-Grounded RAG)

Dense 384-D Vector Retrieval, Verbatim Source Citations & SOP Extraction
Zero Hallucinations
Problem It Solves

Standard AI assistants fabricate facts, invent non-existent papers, and provide unverified answers without traceable sources, making them unusable for real scientific research.

CIPHER Solution: Executes cosine similarity search over your exact document chunks, synthesizes answers strictly from top-matching passages, and provides exact source citations with similarity scores.

How to Use It
1 Go to Knowledge Copilot.
2 Click a preset chip (e.g., Methodology Comparison, Datasets & Accuracy) or type a custom question.
3 Click Ask Copilot to view the answer synthesis and the exact source citations with verbatim quotes.
4 Click Extract SOP Checklist to compile actionable Standard Operating Procedures across all documents.
04

4. Research Intelligence

Deep Scientific Structure Extraction & Cross-Paper Comparative Synthesis Matrix
Multi-Paper Matrix
Problem It Solves

Comparing 10 or 20 academic papers requires hours of manual cross-referencing to find what methodology each paper used, what datasets were tested, and what their limitations were.

CIPHER Solution: Extracts 6 structured dimensions per paper (Methodology, Datasets, Metrics, Findings, Limitations, Future Work) and synthesizes a comparative consensus matrix.

How to Use It
1 Navigate to Research Intelligence.
2 Select any document from the dropdown and click Extract Paper Structure to view its 6-factor scientific breakdown.
3 Click Generate Multi-Paper Comparison Matrix to synthesize findings and compare all papers side-by-side.
05

5. Evidence Explorer

Empirical Claim Extraction, Stance Classification & Contradiction Detection
Fact-Checking
Problem It Solves

Contradictions, flawed assumptions, and unsupported claims across different papers are nearly impossible to catch manually, leading to flawed experimental designs.

CIPHER Solution: Scans passages for assertive claims and evaluates stance relationships (Supports, Contradicts, Neutral) with confidence metrics and semantic similarity.

How to Use It
1 Navigate to Evidence Explorer.
2 Option A (Project Audit): Leave input empty and click Run Evidence Stance Audit to review all mined claims and their cross-document verdicts.
3 Option B (Hypothesis Check): Enter a specific claim (e.g., "Acoustic sensing detects mechanical defects") to see corroborating or contradicting passages.

6. Knowledge Graph Engine

Cytoscape.js Force-Directed Interactive Graph, Entity Clustering & Citation Inspector
Graph Visualization
Problem It Solves

Linear text reading hides multi-hop conceptual relationships, shared datasets between disparate authors, and overarching architectural paradigms.

CIPHER Solution: Renders an interactive 2D physics graph where papers, methods, metrics, and entities form interconnected semantic clusters with traceable sentence citations.

How to Use It
1 Open Knowledge Graph Engine.
2 Zoom with mouse wheel, pan across the canvas, and click any node to highlight its connected relationships.
3 Use the search box or entity filters (Task, Method, Metric, Dataset) to focus on specific domains.
4 Inspect the right-hand panel for exact node properties, confidence scores, and verbatim citations.

7. Multi-Agent Research Collaboration Team

Autonomous 4-Stage Pipeline: Researcher → Reviewer → Experiment Designer → Statistician
Autonomous Team
Problem It Solves

Conducting research in isolation causes bias, overlooking methodology critiques, missing experimental control variables, and failing to verify statistical significance.

CIPHER Solution: Deploys 4 specialized autonomous agents that sequentially research literature, critique methodology, formulate controlled experiments, and compute statistical power.

How to Use It
1 Go to Multi-Agent Research Collaboration Team.
2 Enter your research goal or click a preset chip (e.g., Acoustic Anomaly Detection).
3 Click Launch Collaborative Team. Watch the live progress stepper transition across all 4 agents.
4 Review individual agent dossiers (hypotheses, critiques, test plans, power metrics) and copy or download the final synthesized intelligence report.

8. Report Studio

Publication-Ready Markdown Reports, Executive Briefs, Benchmark Tables & SOP Manuals
Export Engine
Problem It Solves

Transforming complex research findings into polished, publication-ready literature reviews, executive briefs, and deployment protocols takes days of drafting.

CIPHER Solution: Generates complete structured markdown documents grounded in your ingested research, featuring live editing, 1-click clipboard copy, and file downloads.

How to Use It
1 Navigate to Report Studio.
2 Select your format (Comprehensive Literature Review, Executive Decision Brief, Technical Benchmark, SOP Manual).
3 Optionally enter a custom title, then click Generate Report.
4 Edit the markdown draft directly in the editor, and click Copy Markdown or Download .md.

9. NLP Lab (Interactive Playground)

Real-Time Linguistic Parsing, POS Syntax, Emotion Analysis & Cosine Calculator
Linguistic Suite
Problem It Solves

Researchers need to quickly evaluate raw text snippets, verify terminology extractability, check emotional tone, and calculate semantic cosine distance without ingesting whole files.

CIPHER Solution: Provides an interactive sandbox running local spaCy pipelines for entity extraction, POS tagging, salience graph summarization, and cosine distance computation.

How to Use It
1 Open NLP Lab.
2 Paste custom text or click a preset chip (Acoustic Anomaly Paper, Biomedical Note, Financial Call).
3 Adjust the summary length slider and click Execute Deep NLP Analysis to view entities, syntax, and sentiments.
4 Use the Semantic Cosine Distance Calculator at the bottom to test cosine similarity between any two arbitrary sentences.
